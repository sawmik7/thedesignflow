# LinkedIn Outbound Templates — Seed & Series A Startup Founders

These templates are tailored for direct outreach to startup founders who have recently closed a funding round (Seed/Series A) and need execution speed, design polish, or automation scaling.

---

## Variation 1: Premium Brand Identity ($799, 2 weeks)

### Target Persona
Founders whose public presence (website/pitch deck) looks amateur post-funding and need a rapid premium upgrade.

### Connection Request (300 char limit)
> Congrats on the round, [First Name]! 🚀 Growth demands a brand that looks the part. We build premium brand identities ($799, 2 weeks flat) to help post-funding startups build trust with enterprise clients. Worth a quick look at our past work?

### Follow-up Message (If accepted but no reply)
> Hi [First Name],
> 
> Realize you're heads-down post-funding. Most startups waste months and thousands on branding agencies. 
> 
> We deliver a complete premium brand identity (logo, typography, color palette, style guide) in **2 weeks for a flat $799**.
> 
> You can view our design work here: [designflow.website](https://thedesignflow.website)
> 
> Do you have 5 minutes next week to see if we're a fit for your current brand goals?
> 
> Best,
> [Sender Name]
