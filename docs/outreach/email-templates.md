# Cold Email Outreach Templates

These cold email templates target startup founders and product leads, leveraging proven case study metrics and frictionless CTAs.

---

## Variation 1: Product UI/UX & Conversion Focus

### Target Persona
SaaS Founders, Product Leads, VP of Product.

### Subject Lines
* `Quick question about [Company] design`
* `UI/UX redesign at [Company]`
* `38% higher onboarding completion?`

### Body Copy
> Hi [First Name],
> 
> Noticed [Company] recently closed your [Seed/Series A] round — congrats!
> 
> As you scale the product, design bottlenecks or onboarding friction can silently kill your acquisition budget. 
> 
> We design high-converting SaaS UI/UX. For example, we designed **FlowAI's** new user flow which **reduced onboarding drop-offs by 38%** in 30 days.
> 
> We do flat-rate SaaS UI/UX design (including full Figma design systems) for **$1,999**.
> 
> You can check out our recent SaaS design work here: [designflow.website](https://thedesignflow.website)
> 
> Would you be open to a 10-minute feedback call next week? I'd love to share 2-3 ideas we have for [Company]'s onboarding flow.
> 
> Best regards,
> 
> [Sender Name]  
> [Title] | DesignFlow
